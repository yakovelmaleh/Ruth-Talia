The woman behind the label

A new country.
A blank page.
An old dream.

Ruth Talia arrived in Israel from Germany carrying little more than her eye for detail, her love of fabric, and a belief that a dress can change the way a woman enters a room.

Starting again from zero became her greatest creative freedom. She built an atelier around one promise: every woman deserves design that is beautiful, flattering, and genuinely comfortable — without asking her to become someone else.
